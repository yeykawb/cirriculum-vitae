# Glossary

## CV content

- **Employer**: the company Jakob was employed by for a period (Consid, Capgemini, Visiba Care). Experience is grouped by employer and period. The same employer can appear twice for separate periods (Capgemini 2018–2021 and 2022–2023).
- **Assignment**: a client engagement carried out while at a consulting employer (Ambea, Bostadsförmedlingen, Hedin Bil, Volvo Cars). Non-consulting employers have a role instead of assignments.
- **Title**: Jakob's professional title, *Data Platform Engineer*.
- **Outside work**: interests (ice cream science, sourdough) and personal portfolio projects, presented together.

## Visual language

- **Ensō**: the open brush-stroke circle framing the name in the hero, taken from the PDF resume.
- **Ridge**: one layer of misty mountain silhouette. The hero has several, stacked from back (faint) to front (solid).
- **Motif**: a faded fragment of a real, public-domain Japanese artwork used quietly in a section.
- **Day arc**: the story told from top to bottom: the sun sets in the hero, the paper cools slightly, and the page ends under a moon.
- **Hanko**: the small red seal used as a signature mark.
- **Washi**: the off-white paper tone that is the page background.
